I built this Streamlit site after my award-winning experience at the 2025 NODASS Ocean Big Data Competition. It lets me clean up buoy observations, plot charts, run LSTM forecasts, and check wave and wind alerts. I can download the results, too.

What I built and what I can validate

Evaluation and limitations

Backtest metrics are available; an independent test result is not.

The app can show train/validation RMSE, Pearson correlation and epsilon-based hit rate. There is no fixed-dataset final test benchmark yet, so I don't present those scores as verified forecast accuracy.

The current implementation fits the scaler on the full selected time range before splitting it, and optional centered smoothing may use future observations. Both can leak information into validation. I documented these limitations and the need for a train-only scaler and separate test period rather than claiming production forecasting performance.
