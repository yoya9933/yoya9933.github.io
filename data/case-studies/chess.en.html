Online Chinese Chess

I built a Chinese chess website where friends can play together from different devices. There's standard Xiangqi, Jieqi, undo requests, replay and custom board positions. Players can join a game by sharing a room code.

What works and how it's tested

This is a live multiplayer chess site, not just a board UI.

Server-authoritative stateA Worker validates moves, D1 persists state, and Durable Objects/WebSocket notify connected players.

Problems I had to handle

Keeping moves in orderWebSocket announces updates while polling remains a fallback. Revision checks stop older state from overwriting newer moves.
Coming back after a disconnectThe browser saves a player token so someone can rejoin their original seat.
Different game modesThe same board supports regular Xiangqi, Jieqi, sandbox mode and custom setups.
Saving a gameRoom data includes the board state, version, connection status and undo requests.
