04 / Updating content

Admins can edit announcements and products after signing in.

  • Admins sign in with a Supabase email and password.
  • They can add, edit and delete announcements, including categories and tags.
  • They can change product names, prices and categories, and publish or unpublish a product.
  • Images are uploaded to Supabase Storage; product and announcement records go into PostgreSQL.
  • Row Level Security helps control who can access the data.

05 / Tools I used

06 / After the site goes live

Products and announcements keep changing even after a site launches.

That's why this project includes an admin area, not just public pages. The people running the service can keep the information up to date themselves.
